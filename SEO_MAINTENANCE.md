## 🎓 Advanced SEO Tactics

### Semantic SEO
Use related keywords naturally:
- "AI Trainer" → "Artificial Intelligence Trainer"
- "Data Science" → "Data Analytics", "Statistical Modeling"
- "Machine Learning" → "ML Engineering", "Deep Learning"

### Topic Clustering
Group related content:
1. **Generative AI Hub**
   - What is Generative AI
   - ChatGPT for Business
   - Prompt Engineering Course
   
2. **Data Science Hub**
   - Python for Data Science
   - Data Analysis Techniques
   - Data Visualization

3. **Career Development Hub**
   - AI Career Path
   - Skills for 2024

### Internal Linking Strategy
Link from:
- High-authority pages → New content

- Footer navigation → Important pages
